# v0.5.4

- Fegg 5.x docs ([/docs/fegg/next/](/docs/fegg/next/)): every class is under `src/` now, at the path of its namespace. The project's controllers are in `site/src/Controller/`, and the core's controllers and fegg-cli commands are in `core/src/Controller/` and `core/src/Command/` ([Directory structure](/docs/fegg/next/first/cont))
- New page [Dummy content](/docs/fegg/next/basic/cms/dummy): fill a CMS post type with dummy posts outside production, from the CMS screen or with `fegg-cli dummy:*`
- [Rich text (FeggEditor)](/docs/fegg/next/basic/cms/editor): galleries take 2 to 5 columns, and 1 to 3 columns on a phone (`is-sp-cols-*`). List items and table cells hold their text in a `<p>`, and the public styles remove its margins
